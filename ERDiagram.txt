 ER Diagram Structure (Text Version)

This file describes the entities and relationships used in the Game Stats Manager project.

## Entities

1. Team
- TeamID
- TeamName

2. Player
- PlayerID
- PlayerName
- TeamID (FK → Team.TeamID)

3. Match
- MatchID
- MatchDate
- Opponent
- Result
- TeamID (FK → Team.TeamID)

4. Stats
- StatID
- PlayerID (FK → Player.PlayerID)
- MatchID (FK → Match.MatchID)
- PointsScored
- Assists
- Rebounds

## Relationships

- A Team has many Players
- A Team has many Matches
- A Player has many Stats records
- A Match has many Stats records

## Diagram Layout (Text Form)

Team 1 --- ∞ Player
Team 1 --- ∞ Match
Player 1 --- ∞ Stats
Match 1 --- ∞ Stats

## Summary

This ER diagram models how players, teams, matches, and stats connect in a relational database. It supports tracking player performance across multiple games.
